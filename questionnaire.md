# XR project brief (one prompt, one file)

Fill this in, attach `xr-base.html`, and paste the whole thing as your first message. Write in body words, not metres: *arm's reach · near (a step away) · across the room · far* and *floor · knee · waist · chest · eye · overhead*. Sizes: *fingertip · egg · apple · hand · head · torso · person · door · car*. Leave a line blank if you truly don't care and Claude will choose.

## A. The idea
1. One sentence. "You put on the headset and ___."
2. After two minutes, a visitor should understand or feel: ___
3. Tier (pick one):
   - **1 Explore** — look at and walk around something. No interaction beyond moving.
   - **2 Touch** — point-and-select or grab things; each does one thing (glow, grow, speak, play a tone, show text).
   - **3 Rules** — score, turns, order, timing, win/lose. *Only if your interactions in C are simple.*
   For a two-hour session, Tier 1 or 2 is the safe choice. Tier 3 usually needs a second round.

## B. The space
4. Posture: standing in place / standing with teleport / seated.
5. The first thing you see when you arrive (1–3 things). For each: what it is, how big, how far, which direction, how high.
   e.g. *"a head-sized glowing sphere, a step away, straight ahead, at chest height."*
6. Everything else in the space, same format. Group repeated things: *"five pedestals on an arc at arm's reach, waist-high."*
7. Anything deliberately above, below, or behind you? (Otherwise everything goes in front.)

## C. Interactions (max 3; Tier 1 leaves this blank)
For each one, fill all four slots:
- **You do**: point + trigger / grab and move / walk up to / look at for a moment
- **to**: which thing
- **and it**: what changes — visible change, sound, text that appears (write the text)
- **then**: it stays / resets when you let go / resets after a few seconds / happens once only

## D. Rules and state (Tier 3 only)
8. What is counted or tracked, and where it's shown (e.g. *"strokes, on a sign across the room at eye level"*).
9. What ends it, and what the visitor sees when it ends.
10. How to start over.

## E. Look, feel, words
11. Three words for the mood. Colours: name up to three from *red orange yellow green teal blue purple pink white grey dark*, or "you choose".
12. Any text that must appear exactly as written (title, labels, instructions).

## F. Scope guard
13. Out of scope — things you're explicitly not asking for (e.g. *"no realistic physics, no scoring, no sound"*).
14. If something has to be cut to make it work today, cut this first: ___
15. Don't change: ___ (e.g. *"keep the grid floor"*)

### What you get without asking (don't spend words on these)
Metric world with the floor at floor level and you at the centre facing forward. Controllers with a pointing ray, trigger to select, thumbstick-forward to teleport, thumbstick-sideways to turn. Hover glow on anything interactive. A ground plane, sky, lighting, a 1 m grid. Text that is sized to be readable at its distance and always faces you. Everything placed at "eye" or "chest" height adjusts to the real wearer's height a moment after the session starts. Desktop preview with mouse and keyboard. Errors appear as red text on screen. Shapes are built from primitives (boxes, balls, cylinders, cones, rings, flat panels, lines) — no downloaded models, images, fonts or sounds. Sounds are simple synthesized tones. No physics engine: things move only the way your answers in C describe. One person, no multiplayer.
