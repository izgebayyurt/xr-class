**The idea I want to teach:** Which is bigger: 5/9 or 4/7?

**What I already know about it:** you find a common denominator. 5/9 is 35/63 and 4/7 is 36/63 so 4/7 is bigger. or turn them into decimals

**What my friend would get wrong at first, and why:** they think 5/9 is bigger because 5 and 9 are bigger numbers

**How I would show my friend why the right answer is right:** two chocolate bars the same size, one cut in 9 pieces and one cut in 7, you take 5 and 4 pieces and put them next to each other

**What happens first, next, and at the end:** first guess which is bigger, then cut the bars, then compare, then try other fractions

**Look and feel:** chocolate, cozy

**Flat or 3D:** Flat (2D)
