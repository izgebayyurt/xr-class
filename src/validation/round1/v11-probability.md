## First impression (start screen only)
What is this about? Probability with two dice: which sum (2 to 12) comes up most often when you add them.
What would you do first? Click one of the four guess buttons; I'd pick "Sum 7".

## After playing
What is it about / what was it trying to teach? Sums of two dice aren't equally likely: 7 has 6 of the 36 red+blue combinations and 2 or 12 have only 1, so with many rolls the histogram turns into a triangle. You can compare your simulated rolls with the "ways" block chart.

## Scores (1–5)
- clear goal: 5
- clear first action: 5
- could finish it: 4
- shows WHY (5 = I could inspect the reasoning myself; 1 = it just told/animated the answer): 4
- looks right on a laptop (everything needed is visible, readable, nothing overlapping/hidden): 2

## Problems
- The "Roll the dice" / "6 + 6 = 12" sum label floats on top of the instruction panel's last line, covering words ("Ro… : 1", "Click a c… of blocks").
- The center panel covers the inner edges of both charts. Before rolling, the right end of the histogram (12 and its count) sits behind it. After rolling, the sum-2 column of the "ways" chart ("1+1" and its count "1") is half hidden.
- Both charts are small and far away. The "1+3"-style labels on the blocks and the "white line = expected" markers are barely readable at 1280×800.
- The guess is never checked. After I picked "Sum 7" and 100 rolls came out with 5 on top, nothing related that result to my guess or said that 100 rolls is noisy. The panel only reported "most common is 5".
- Clicking a column is the main "why" interaction, but the blocks are tiny. When I clicked the chart, it picked the sum-2 column rather than the one I aimed at, so choosing a specific column is fiddly. By label I could only reach the whole chart, not "3+4".
- The instructions say "Click Roll 1, then Roll 100", but there is also an unexplained "Roll 1000" button.

## Best moment
Clicking a column showed "Sum 2: 1 of 36 ways (2.8%) … your rolls 5.0% … 7 has 6: expect 7 about 6x as often", which ties the block chart to the rolled data.
