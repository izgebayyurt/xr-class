## First impression (start screen only)
What is this about? Why some notes sound good together: whether it's just taste, habit, or something physical about the sound.
What would you do first? Click a couple of piano keys to hear them, then click the "Sound waves" pad.

## After playing
What is it about / what was it trying to teach? Consonance comes from frequency ratios. You pick two keys, it plays them and draws both waves plus their sum, with white lines where the waves line up. Simple ratios (like C+G) line up often and sound smooth, while complex ones (F+B) don't.

- clear goal: 4

- shows WHY (5 = I could inspect the reasoning myself; 1 = it just told/animated the answer): 3
- looks right on a laptop (everything needed is visible, readable, nothing overlapping/hidden): 2

## Problems
- On the start screen the three guess pads ("Just taste / Learned habit / Sound waves") sit on top of the keyboard and cover most of the keys you're told to click first. The keys show only as tall grey slabs with tiny tags (C, D, E…).
- The wave panel runs off the top of the screen. Its first label ("C 262 Hz") and the top wave sit under the desktop-preview hint box, so the top of the main "why" picture is clipped.
- A developer badge, "Scene check: 0 errors · 6 warnings — show report", is visible to the student in the bottom-left corner.
- There's no ending. The guess is never answered ("You guessed: Learned habit" and then nothing ever says whether that was right), and there's no summary after trying pairs, so I couldn't tell when I was done.
- Clicking keys before guessing gives no visible feedback (no highlight or text), so on a muted laptop it looks like nothing happened.
- For C + C the "both together" wave looks the same as each single wave, with the same height. That's a confusing first example of "adding" waves.
- (Tool limit) Every key click landed on C, so I only ever got C + C (1:1). I couldn't try the suggested C+G or F+B pairs, so I never saw the contrast between a smooth and a rough pair.

## Best moment
The C + C wave panel: two coloured waves, their sum, and white alignment lines labelled "1:1 … line up every 1 cycle". It looks like a real tool for checking the claim yourself.
