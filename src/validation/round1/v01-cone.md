## First impression (start screen only)
What is this about? Comparing the volume of a cone and a cylinder with the same base and height, using sand. What would you do first? Click one of the four guess boards ("1 cone" … "4 cones") — the panel says to.

## After playing
What is it about / what was it trying to teach? That a cone holds 1/3 of a cylinder with the same base and height: you pour cones of sand until the cylinder is full (3 pours), then slice both into layers and use a slider to see the cone's share stay near 1/3 (0.324 at 3 layers, 0.332 at 8, 0.333 at 20), tying it to V = 1/3 × base area × height.

- clear goal: 5

- looks right on a laptop (everything needed is visible, readable, nothing overlapping/hidden): 4

## Problems
- The layers step doesn't really show why it's 1/3. The slider only changes one ratio label, and that label is already about 1/3 at 3 layers (0.324), so nothing visibly converges. You can't see any layer's own volume, so you just have to take the number on trust.
- Twice in about 8 runs a pour click didn't register and the count stayed at 2. Retrying fixed it. This might be my click timing during the pour animation, but a student would think the scene had stalled.
- The 1/3 conclusion only appears after the last pour animation ends (about 3 s later). If you click again before then, nothing happens and there's no hint to wait.
- The "Start over" and "Show layers" buttons are tiny blue rectangles, and their white text spills well past the button on both sides. It's hard to tell what you can click.
- Your guess gets no feedback until the end. The guess boards just stay grey, and only after the third pour do they turn red (your guess) and green (the answer).
- With 3 layers, the bottom cone layer (a thin pink cylinder) pokes out below the cone's tip.
- The panel text "Model: each pour adds one cone's volume; the level is volume / base area" is jargon to a newcomer and isn't needed to play.

## Best moment
Watching the sand level jump 33% → 67% → 100% after exactly three pours, then seeing my wrong guess turn red next to the green "3 cones".
