## First impression (start screen only)
What is this about? The two sides of the heart: where the right side pumps blood (lungs vs body). There's a simple heart model with a "Lungs" disc above and a "Body" disc below. What would you do first? Click one of the three guess answers next to the panel. They're hard to read, though: tiny grey tabs with big text spilling around them.

## After playing
What is it about / what was it trying to teach? The right ventricle pumps only to the lungs and the left ventricle pumps to the whole body, which is why the left wall is thicker (about 10 mm vs 4 mm). You follow one blood cell around the full loop: body → right atrium → right ventricle → lungs (turns red) → left atrium → left ventricle → body.

## Problems
- The answer buttons are broken-looking. "Same as the left", "Lungs only" and "Whole body" are tiny grey rectangles with oversized text wrapping one word per line around and past them, and "Lungs" runs into "Whole". It's unclear what to click and which label belongs to which button.
- The tour is very slow. One loop took about 2 minutes on my laptop (around 15 s per stage), with no way to speed up or skip. Until the end the panel shows a one-sentence caption per stage. Part of this may be my machine (a "4-second" timer in another scene also ran slow), but even at full speed it's a passive watch.
- The thick-wall explanation is only stated. The final panel says the left wall is 2.5× thicker, but on the model the two ventricle walls (the pink rims) look about the same. You can't see or measure the difference, and nothing lets you test what a thin wall would do.
- In the final state the panel grows and covers the "Start over" button. Only "over" is readable and "Start" sits under the panel text.
- "Click any chamber to see its name": the chambers have no labels until the very end, so you have to know they're clickable. My tool couldn't click them, so I didn't see the chamber names before the tour.
- The question asks about the heart's RIGHT side, but the right side is on the viewer's left (correct anatomically). Nothing marks this before the tour, so a newcomer may think the red side on the right is the "right side".
- A yellow "Scene check: 0 errors · 8 warnings — show report" developer badge is visible in the bottom-left corner.
- While the cell is inside a chamber, it's a pale, barely visible blob.

## Best moment
Watching the blue cell go up to the lungs and come back red, which shows the right side only ever sends blood to the lungs.
