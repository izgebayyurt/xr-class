## First impression (start screen only)
What is this about? Whether a pendulum's mass affects how fast it swings: a 2 kg and a 0.5 kg bob on equal strings, both pulled back 20°.
What would you do first? Click a guess (A faster / B faster / Same). I'd guess "Same".

## After playing
What is it about / what was it trying to teach? A pendulum's period doesn't depend on its mass: both bobs swing and both measure a period of 1.28 s on a 0.40 m string.

## Scores (1–5)
- clear goal: 5
- clear first action: 5
- could finish it: 4
- shows WHY (5 = I could inspect the reasoning myself; 1 = it just told/animated the answer): 2
- looks right on a laptop (everything needed is visible, readable, nothing overlapping/hidden): 4

## Problems
- The guess is never answered. "A faster", "B faster" and "Same" all lead to the same "Released. Counting swings…" screen, and once both periods show 1.28 s there's no "you were right/wrong" and no takeaway sentence. It just stops, and you have to work out the conclusion yourself from two identical numbers.
- It only shows the result, not the reason. There's nothing to change afterwards (no length, mass or angle control, no second trial), so I can't test what *does* change the period or see why mass doesn't matter.
- The two pendulums sit at opposite edges of the screen (far left and far right), so it's hard to watch them side by side and see they're in step. The comparison relies on the text numbers.
- "period measuring..." sits there for several seconds with no progress indicator or swing counter, so it looks stuck at first.
- After release the guess buttons disappear, so there's no record of what I picked when the result arrives.

## Best moment
Both readouts landing on exactly "period 1.28 s" for the 2 kg and 0.5 kg bobs, a clean surprise if you guessed the heavy one would be faster.
