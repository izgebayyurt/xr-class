## First impression (start screen only)
What is this about? Imagery in poetry: six short phrases (lamplight, kettle hum, cold glass, cinnamon, honey, rain on tin) each tied to a sense.
What would you do first? Answer the question of how many of the six are about seeing by clicking Most / Some / Few. I'd pick "Few: 0-1", since only the lamplight is visual.

## After playing
What is it about / what was it trying to teach? Sensory imagery: classify each phrase by the sense it calls on, and compare it with a "plain version" (e.g. "gold lamplight" vs. just "light") to see that imagery lets you experience a thing, not just name it.

## Problems
- I couldn't get past 1 of 6. Every phrase I tried to tap (kettle, cold glass, cinnamon, honey, rain) registered as one big clickable object made of the panel plus all six phrases, and the click always landed on "gold lamplight". Tapping "rain ticking on tin" first still revealed lamplight. The individual phrases/spheres don't seem to be separate targets, so the activity is stuck at "1 of 6 tapped" and the guess is never checked.
- The phrase labels are very small (around 7–8 px for "cold glass in my palm", "cinnamon on the air") and hard to read on a laptop.
- "Guess locked in." doesn't say what I guessed, and the guess pads don't change colour, so after a few taps there's no reminder.
- The text says the six images "drift around you", but they sit in a static row in front of you. It's a minor mismatch, but it makes you look around for things that aren't there.
- The plain white spheres don't hint at their sense until tapped (lamplight turns gold), so there's nothing to look at besides the labels.

## Best moment
Tapping "gold lamplight": the sphere turns warm gold and the panel contrasts it with the plain word "light": "The image lets you see it; the plain words only name it."
